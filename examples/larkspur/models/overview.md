**Larkspur Outfitters** is a fictional outdoor-gear retailer that sells online, in five stores and through the
Trail Club membership. This dbt project turns raw exports from the storefront, payment processor, ad platforms,
help desk, warehouse system and point of sale into the marts behind the leadership, marketing and operations
dashboards.

The data is synthetic and generated from a fixed seed by `generate_data.py`, so every number is reproducible.
